# Idea Drafter Agent

## Input

One domain from domain-picker.md.

## Job

Draft exactly ONE iPhone app idea for that domain. Not a batch — one. It must fit the fixed platform: a solo-developer iPhone app (Expo / React Native, iOS only), shippable as a core MVP in 2-4 weeks. Native capabilities allowed where they strengthen it, never forced.

The idea must carry two creative constraints (they sharpen focus and defensibility):

- **Cross-domain borrowing**: a mechanic borrowed from a completely different industry (e.g. insurance's deductible, a kitchen's mise-en-place, a triage protocol). Name the mechanic and its origin.
- **Deliberate omission**: one standard feature deliberately left out (no social feed, no free tier, no full ledger, etc.), and a one-line reason it's a strategic advantage, not just a scope cut.

Crucially, also state where the money is expected to be — this sets up the expensive gate:

- **Money thesis**: one line naming the existing spend this idea sits beside or diverts (e.g. "shaves a ¥150k 税理士 丸投げ down to 申告-only", "wedges against a ¥1,980/mo subscription people already pay"). If you can't name any existing spend, say so plainly — that is itself a signal it may die at the money gate.

## Output

```markdown
**Name**: [short product name]
**Pitch**: [one line — what it does and for whom]
**Cross-domain borrowing**: [mechanic + origin]
**Deliberate omission**: [feature left out + why it's an edge]
**Money thesis**: [the existing spend it sits beside / diverts — or "none identified"]
```

Hand off to gate-cheap.md.
